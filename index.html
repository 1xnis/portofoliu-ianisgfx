About me

I'm Ianis.
One idea goes in,
a whole spectrum comes out.

Designer, developer and editor from Romania. I turn raw ideas into visuals, websites and edits with character.

  • Detail

    Every pixel, every frame and every line of code gets the same attention. I look closely until nothing feels accidental: spacing, timing, contrast. The small things are what make a project feel truly finished.

  • Quality

    Work that looks and feels premium, delivered on time. Clear steps, honest feedback and files ready for print, web or social media. No surprises, just a result you're proud to show.

  • Feeling

    Design people remember for how it makes them feel. Every visual, website or edit starts from one question: what should someone feel in the first three seconds? Everything else is built around that answer.
